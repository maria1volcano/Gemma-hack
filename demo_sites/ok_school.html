Homework tips that actually help

  • Work for twenty minutes, then stretch for five. Repeat twice and you are done.
  • Read the question out loud before you start writing. It catches a lot of mistakes.
  • Keep one notebook for everything so nothing gets lost between subjects.
  • If you are stuck for more than ten minutes, ask a teacher or a grown-up at home.
  • Pack your bag the night before. Mornings are calmer that way.
